About

East Street · Seal Bay · Selsey

A one-person service, on foot.

Palmer’s Pet Care walks dogs from the owner’s door, visits cats and small pets in their own houses, and stays overnight when you go away.

I do not drive and I do not board dogs at my house. The work stays local: East Street, Seal Bay, the High Street, East Beach and streets I can reach in about fifteen minutes.

A free meet and greet always comes first. No keys and no paid walk until we have met you and your pet.
